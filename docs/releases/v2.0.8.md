# FlowDeck v2.0.8

## Fixed & Hardened

- **Continuation-Prompt Flood Guard**: Eliminated the control-plane defect where automated FlowDeck recovery prompts were mistaken for manual user interactions and repeatedly reset recovery budgets.
- **Centralized RecoveryCoordinator**: Enforces strictly at most one pending automatic continuation per session (`pendingAutoContinuations <= 1`) across all sources.
- **Strict Internal Prompt Provenance**: Internal FlowDeck recovery prompts (`REPLAY_CONTINUATION_PROMPT`, watchdog prompts) are tracked by generation and message ID, ensuring only verified external human turns can retire exhausted incidents.
- **Watchdog & Reasoning Deduplication**: Semantic watchdog recovery now routes through the central coordinator and is automatically suppressed when reasoning recovery is already scheduled or running.
- **Tool Lifecycle Protection**: In-progress and pending tool calls are properly recognized during history safety evaluation, preventing false empty-output recovery triggers during active tool execution.
- **Session Stop & Cancellation Cleanup**: Cancelling or stopping a session immediately purges scheduled continuation timers, internal prompt records, and pending flags.
